# Code of Conduct

CareerPilot follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- Protect private or sensitive information.
- Focus disagreements on ideas and evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption,
and publishing another person's private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile contact channel.
Maintainers will acknowledge reports promptly, preserve confidentiality, investigate fairly, and
apply proportionate corrective action. Maintainers who are involved in a report must recuse
themselves.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
