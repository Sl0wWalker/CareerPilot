# Maintainers

CareerPilot currently uses a benevolent-maintainer governance model.

## Roles

- **Maintainer:** merges changes, manages releases, handles security reports, and approves ADRs.
- **Reviewer:** provides domain-specific review and may approve but not merge protected changes.
- **Contributor:** proposes changes through issues and pull requests.

Two approvals are recommended for security, authentication, plugin permissions, data migration,
and automatic-submission changes. At least one maintainer approval is required for release and
governance changes.

Maintainer nominations consider sustained high-quality contributions, sound privacy and safety
judgment, respectful collaboration, and availability. Inactive maintainers may move to emeritus
status after six months without project activity.
